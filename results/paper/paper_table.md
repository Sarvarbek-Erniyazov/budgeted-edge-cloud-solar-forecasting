All rows are on the primary rows (39,655 cells). RMSE is averaged over the 6 horizons. For the edge, cloud and gate rows it is the mean over 5 seeds, with the range over seeds in brackets; the other rows are deterministic. Share kept = (RMSE_edge - RMSE) / (RMSE_edge - RMSE_cloud), from the seed-mean RMSEs, with the edge tier in fp32; n/a for the anchors. Realised rate is the share of issue times escalated (0 for the edge tier, 1 for the cloud tier, n/a where nothing is escalated). The gate rows use a 25% target budget; random is the expected random gate. Size is in kB (1 kB = 1,000 B). The tree sizes are of different kinds: the 10x-capped trees are a compiled C object, and trees on all inputs are a Python pickle. Cloud-tier size is not stated. Latency is the median single-row, one-thread time on a PC CPU (ONNX Runtime or compiled C); nothing ran on a microcontroller. Gate size and latency are in addition to the edge tier.
